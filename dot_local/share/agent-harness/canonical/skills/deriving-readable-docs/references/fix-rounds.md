# Fix rounds (steps 10, 13 and 15 of SKILL.md)

Round 1 is the checkers' reports on the whole guide. Each later round re-checks only the sentences that changed. At most 2 fix rounds after round 1.

## Who fixes what

- Appendix findings: the lead edits the section files itself, then runs `stamp.py` on them. A line keeps its id, so the Part 1 tags that point at it hold.
- A fix that splits a sentence splits it. It does not paste the checker's wording: that breaks the 18-word limit.
- A Part 1 finding is fixed by a fresh front-writer session. When the finding is a missing condition, Part 1 cannot state what its tagged appendix line lacks, so the lead also puts the condition on that appendix line, in the same round.
- After fix round 2 the lead narrows each sentence that still fails by the repair rule below, a Part 1 sentence included. Step 13 checks the whole guide again.

## The repair rule (after round 2, and step 13)

A finding is repaired, not deleted. An OVERSTATED claim, or one missing a condition (MISSING_CONDITION), gets the condition the checker names added: split the sentence if it passes 18 words, and keep each part's condition. A NAME or COUNT finding gets the value or word the code shows. A WRONG claim is rewritten to the true statement the code shows when that helps a reader. Cut a claim only when it is wrong and no true version helps a reader, or when nothing in the cited lines supports it (UNSUPPORTED) and no line does. Edit the section files and `part1.md`, never `GUIDE.md`, and run `assemble.py`, `check_citations.py` and `lint_main.py` again. Nothing re-checks the step 13 repairs until step 15.

## Part 1 over 1,000 words

`lint_main.py` fails Part 1 over 1,000 words at every step, fix rounds included. A fix that adds a condition grows a sentence: cut to fit in this order. First shorten or merge the sentences a fix narrowed (a merged sentence keeps every condition its tags state, and the 18-word limit). Then drop a What you can do line the appendix covers in full. Never cut How to start or the Who it is for sentence. A sentence a coverage pass added to Part 1 (`references/coverage.md`) goes first: a coverage sentence that still fails after one fix is dropped and its fact stays in the appendix.

## The commands, in order

1. Rerun the step 9 script (`check_citations.py` with the budget flags) on the fixed guide. A fix can break a citation or the budget, and `slice.py` reads the guide as it stands.
2. List the changed ids in `changed.txt` and run `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/slice.py GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed.txt --out slices-fixN --was <the guide as last cut>`. It cuts Part 1 and the appendix apart, each into slices of near-equal size. With `--was`, it also picks up a line a fix split, reworded or moved, found by comparing its text with the old guide as well as its id.
3. One fresh checker per slice, with `references/check-brief.md`, reports into `reports-fixN`.
4. `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/slice.py GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed.txt --was <the guide as last cut> --reports reports-fixN --record-fixed fixed-ids.txt`. Give the same `--was` as in step 2, or a sentence that only `--was` picked up is not recorded. A Part 1 sentence whose re-check says HOLDS is recorded as it stands, and any other as `cut`. `lint_main.py --fixed fixed-ids.txt` then warns when a front writer rewords a recorded sentence, and skips a `cut` row.
5. Score the round's reports against that round's own slices folder: `score.py <name>=reports-fixN --doc GUIDE.md --repo <repo> --slices slices-fixN`. Its words and rates are those of the re-checked sentences.

## Final re-check (step 15 of SKILL.md)

The step 13 repairs, the reader check's front-writer fix, and any line a coverage pass added after the full check change sentences after the last checker report. This step checks each of them, repairs what a fix can repair, and cuts the rest. It is not a second pass over the guide. "The guide as last cut" is the copy of GUIDE.md you keep each time you cut slices (`GUIDE.fixN.cut.md`, and `GUIDE.full.cut.md` for step 13): the guide the last checkers saw. Here it is `GUIDE.full.cut.md`, so every claim new or changed since the full check is checked.

A line that fails for a missing condition, a wrong name or a wrong count gets one fix and one re-check before it may be cut. Only a line that fails for another cause, or that still fails its re-check, is cut.

1. `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/final_recheck.py changed GUIDE.md <repo> --was <the guide as last cut> --out changed.txt` lists every claim whose sentence, tags or cited lines differ from that copy, or that moved (the same test as `slice.py --was`). If it says nothing changed, stop: the step is done.
2. `slice.py GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed.txt --was <the guide as last cut> --out slices-final`, one fresh checker per slice with `references/check-brief.md`, reports into `reports-final`, then `score.py <name>=reports-final --doc GUIDE.md --repo <repo> --slices slices-final`. Score before any edit: a claim the checker skipped shows there, and `repair` and `cut` refuse it.
3. `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/final_recheck.py repair GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed.txt --reports reports-final --out repair.txt` lists each claim that is not HOLDS as `fix` (cause MISSING_CONDITION, NAME or COUNT) or `cut` (any other cause), and writes the `fix` ids to `repair.txt`.
4. `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/final_recheck.py cut GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed.txt --reports reports-final --except repair.txt --held-out held.txt --clean` cuts every claim that is not HOLDS except the ones in `repair.txt`. An appendix claim loses its line and a Part 1 claim its sentence. A Part 1 sentence tagged to a cut line loses that tag, and is cut when it has no tag left. It writes GUIDE.md in place and `GUIDE.clean.md`, and `held.txt`: the content of every claim the checkers passed, so step 6 does not check it again. Do not run `assemble.py` again from here: it would bring the cut text back from the section files.
5. Fix each claim of `repair.txt` once, by editing GUIDE.md in place: the lead for an appendix line (keep its id; run `stamp.py GUIDE.md` when a fix split a line), a fresh front-writer session (`Seat: lead (skill writer), Opus 5.5 medium`) for a Part 1 sentence. A missing condition is added as the checker names it (split the sentence past 18 words, keep each part's condition); a name or count takes the value the code shows. A condition added to a Part 1 sentence is added to its tagged appendix line in the same pass. A claim with no true fix is left as it is: step 6 cuts it. Rerun `check_citations.py` (the step 9 command) and `lint_main.py` on GUIDE.md; the Part 1 failures it lists are fixed in this same pass, once, by the same front writer.
6. `final_recheck.py changed GUIDE.md <repo> --was <the guide as last cut> --skip held.txt --out changed2.txt` lists every claim still different from that copy that no checker passed as it stands: the claims just fixed, and the ones left in `repair.txt` whether or not anyone edited them. Every claim the `--except` list kept is re-checked or cut, edited or not. One fresh checker per slice (`slice.py ... --ids changed2.txt --was <the guide as last cut> --out slices-final2`, reports into `reports-final2`, scored against `slices-final2`), then `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/final_recheck.py cut GUIDE.md <repo> --ids changed2.txt --reports reports-final2 --clean`. A repaired claim that still is not HOLDS is cut now.
7. Rerun `check_citations.py` and `lint_main.py`. The shipped guide must pass `lint_main.py`. A Part 1 sentence it fails (a cut can leave a dangling "unless", or a tag that no longer backs the sentence) goes back to the front writer for one narrowing fix: remove the word, the clause or the dead tag, and add no claim, number or condition. A sentence step 5 already fixed for the same failure skips the fix. Then `lint_main.py` runs again, and a sentence that still fails is dropped whole: list its ids in `dropped.txt` and run `python3 -I <skill>/scripts/final_recheck.py drop GUIDE.md <repo> --ids dropped.txt --clean`. `drop` refuses the `Who it is for` sentence and the last sentence of How to start: those get their one fix, and a failure that remains goes in the report. Run `lint_main.py` once more after the drop.
8. Nothing else is rewritten after step 7, and `GUIDE.clean.md` is written by the last `final_recheck.py` command (`--clean`).
